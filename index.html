The name goes in the steel, not on a sticker

Stickers peel. Vinyl lifts in a car that has been sitting outside since ten in the morning. An engraving is material taken out of the surface, so it lasts exactly as long as the cup does.

Write it in Arabic or in Latin letters. A name, a nickname only two people use, a plate number. It is your cup, and we do not have opinions about what goes on it.

Steel does not remember.

The wall your drink touches is stainless steel. It does not hold on to what you drank yesterday, and it does not soften the way plastic does when something hot goes into it every day.

Between the two walls there is a vacuum. Nothing in there to carry heat in, nothing to carry cold out. That is not a claim about our cup — that is what a vacuum wall is.

The Kuwait Heat Test

Ice in the cup at seven in the morning. Cup in a car in Shuwaikh, windows up. A reading every hour until it fails.
