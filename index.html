A colour is not yours. A name is.

Anyone can buy the same colour as you. That is what a colour is for.

An engraving is the one thing about a cup that cannot be bought twice. It is also the reason a cup comes home, instead of getting left on a desk, in a car, at a diwaniya.

What that gets you

  • No leaks in the tote.
  • No sweat ring on the desk.
  • No lid that smells like yesterday’s coffee.
  • No arguing over whose cup this is.

Steel does not remember.

The wall your drink touches is stainless steel. It does not hold on to what you drank yesterday, and it does not soften the way plastic does when something hot goes into it every day.

Between the two walls there is a vacuum. Nothing in there to carry heat in, nothing to carry cold out. That is not a claim about our cup — that is what a vacuum wall is.

The Kuwait Heat Test

Ice in the cup at seven in the morning. Cup in a car in Shuwaikh, windows up. A reading every hour until it fails.

We publish the hour it fails, too. A test you only half publish is an advert.

The test has not been run yet, so there are no numbers on this page. When it is run, the whole log goes here — the hours it held, and the hour it stopped.

Does it fit your car?

A cup that will not sit in the holder is a cup you stop carrying. We are testing cars one at a time and publishing what we find, including the ones it does not fit.
